Say where this app sits, what it replaces, and confirm the department it was already filed under.

## What you will be asked

- It files under the department shown, with that department's charter. Keep it there, or move it?
- What do you do today without it? (filled in; correct it)
- Does it replace or extend something you already have? Name it, or say nothing. (filled in; correct it)
- Only if you want it: anything you would want to track about it over time?

## What it produces

The Strategy rows of the record. No code and no second file.

## Rules in plain words

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| The department is stated, never interviewed | the chat shows where the app is filed and offers keep or move; a move is a structured change, not a hand edit |
| The charter text is quoted from the department, never written by the chat | if the department has no charter yet, the row says so |
| "Replaces" names an existing app or a plain-words thing, or says nothing | naming an existing app offers to archive it |
| Review is a date and only a date | nothing gets scheduled, no reminder, no meeting |
| Publishing is a yes, no or not sure | nobody asks for version numbers, authors or licenses; the app never writes a publish block itself |

## What "done" checks here

Nothing in this angle blocks "done" in this version. The department match, the review date and the publish rule are suggestions the check prints.
