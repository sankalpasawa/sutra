# Product

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **status** | apps frameworks kit, product angle |
| **updated** | 2026-09-12 |

Before any file exists, say who opens this app, what it is called, what it must show tomorrow for it to have worked, what goes wrong, and who keeps it.

## What you will be asked

- Who opens this, and what are they trying to get done?
- Say it in one line someone else would understand. What should it be called?
- You open it tomorrow. What do you see that tells you it worked?
- What is the worst wrong thing it could show, what should it show instead, and what do you do then?
- Walk me through using it once, start to finish. (the chat fills this in from your answers; correct it)
- Who fixes it when it breaks, and what date should it be looked at again? (filled in; correct it)
- At the very end: is anyone besides you going to use this?

## What it produces

The Product rows of the app's record (`APP.md`), plus the app's name and one-line description. Nothing else.

## Rules in plain words

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| One line names the app in words the reader already has | it becomes the name (80 characters) and the line under it (140) |
| Success is something you can see when the app opens | "it works", "done" and "no errors" are not answers |
| The failure answer says what you see and what you do | a denial is not an answer |
| A thing to look at is a page, a conversation is a chat, a shortcut to a screen is a link | the kind is picked before the chat opens; there is no fourth kind |
| Every app names an owner and a review date | archiving is the end state; nothing is ever deleted |
| A page or a chat stays a draft until its success line is true | a link is ready the moment it is created |
| If only you use it, nobody asks about positioning, pricing or growth | those questions never come up by default |
| One app, one job | if the first answer has two jobs in it, it is two apps |

## What "done" checks here

The record exists with every required row answered, the name and the line under it are present and different from each other, and nothing a person reads carries system jargon. Everything else here is a suggestion.
