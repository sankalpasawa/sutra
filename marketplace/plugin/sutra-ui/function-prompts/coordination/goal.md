What Coordination is for, and the rules it keeps.

## does

keeps what engines share: one run at a time, who goes first when several are ready, who may post to whom, the chain's limit, a thread's bounds, and the alarm. Its own agent says who goes first when several functions are ready at once

## skills

- keep one run at a time
- say who goes first
- check who may post what to whom
- stop a chain at its limit
- close a thread at its bound
- raise an alarm
- say who goes first among equals
