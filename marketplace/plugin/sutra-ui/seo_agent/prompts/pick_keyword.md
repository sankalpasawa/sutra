## How to choose

Weigh four things, in this order:
1. Intent match. The keyword must be one the {{INTENT}} reader actually types.
2. Winnable difficulty. A lower-difficulty keyword with decent volume beats a big one
   this site cannot rank for.
3. Volume. Real demand, not a term nobody searches.
4. Distance from what the site already ranks for. Do not pick something that cannibalises
   an existing page.

Never pick a keyword this company already ranks in the top 20 for.

Then pick 4 to 8 secondary keywords from the same candidate list: close variants and
sub-questions the article can also answer. They must be keywords from the list above.
