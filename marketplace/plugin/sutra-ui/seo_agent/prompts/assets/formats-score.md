# The ideas

{{IDEAS}}

# `beatability`, 1 to 3 — how easy it is to make the asset itself worth linking to

There is no competitor page to measure against here. Judge the build, not the field.

- **3** — a simple shape: a glossary, a listicle, a basic calculator. Short build, fast win.
- **2** — moderate: an interactive tool, a data-light index, a definitive guide.
- **1** — heavy: a proprietary data report, a complex interactive, a full microsite.

# `effort`, S / M / L

- **S** — days to a week. A glossary entry, one calculator, a stats roundup.
- **M** — weeks. A thirty-term glossary section, an index on limited data, a definitive guide plus template.
- **L** — a month or more. Annual research, a full microsite, a custom-built interactive.
