# Write exactly this shape, nothing else

```
# Brand scope — {{BRAND}}

## What we are
One paragraph. What the company actually does, in a practitioner's words.

## Subjects we own
Five to nine bullets. A subject we could publish the definitive piece on and be believed.
Each bullet says WHY we have standing: our data, our customers, our product, our position.

## Subjects we do NOT own
Five to nine bullets. Adjacent things a reader might assume, and why they are not ours.
Be specific and be willing to give things up. This half of the document does the real work.

## What we have that others do not
Three to six bullets. The raw material an asset could be built on: aggregate data we hold,
customers who would talk, a measurement only we can make.

## Who would cite us
Two to four bullets. The kind of writer who would link to something we published, and about what.
```

Rules:
- Every claim traces to something above. If the product pages do not say it, do not write it.
- No hype. No "leading", "innovative", "cutting-edge".
- "Subjects we do NOT own" must be genuinely restrictive. A scope that rules nothing out is not a
  scope, and it will cause the judges to approve everything.
- Return the document only. No preamble.
