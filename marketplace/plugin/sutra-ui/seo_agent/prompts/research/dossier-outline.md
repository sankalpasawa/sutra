research found, grouped so a writer can find it. It is read by a machine and by one editor.

RULES
- Between 4 and 8 sections. Fewer if the evidence is thin.
- Every question must belong to exactly one section. Nothing is dropped and nothing is in two.
- Name each section for what the evidence in it SAYS, not the topic it is near. "Agency fees run
  15-25% of first-year salary" beats "Agency fees".
- Costs, failures, limits and who a method disadvantages get their own section when the evidence
  supports one. Never fold them into a section that argues the opposite.
- Stay inside the world the brief names. Where this subject shares a word with a different world,
  no section belongs to that other world.
