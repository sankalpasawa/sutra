FILE 2: VOICES FROM THE FIELD
What real people said about this topic in public forums. Only here when there was something worth
finding.

THIS IS WHAT STOPS THE SECTION READING LIKE AN ENCYCLOPEDIA. Your cards tell you what is true. This
tells you what people actually say, worry about and argue over, and a line of that is what makes a
section sound like a person wrote it rather than a database.

WRITE IT AS WHAT PEOPLE SAY, NOT AS FACT. Then it stays true even when the opinion is not: "the
complaint you hear most often is that...", "in practice this usually falls apart at...", "most teams
will tell you...". Never "research shows". Never a number from this file. Never a [c...] tag on it.
Never quote or name a person.

One or two lines of this in a section is plenty. Where it says "strong", that only means several people
said it, not that it is true. Angry forums make rare problems look common, so take the point and leave
the mood.
