Writing rules. These are not suggestions.

- No em dashes. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop instead.
- Never use these words: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, landscape, realm,
  testament, underscore, navigate (in the figurative sense), tapestry, crucial,
  vital, moreover, furthermore.
- Never open with "In today's world", "In the ever-evolving", "In an era of", or
  any variant of them.
- Vary sentence length deliberately. Put a short sentence next to a long one.
- Lead with the point, then explain it. No throat-clearing before the answer.
- Never invent a statistic, a study, a date, a company or a source. If you do not
  have a real number, write the sentence without one.
