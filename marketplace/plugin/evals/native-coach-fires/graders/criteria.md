PASS if the answer does all five:

1. States the founder's idea as one claim that a result could prove wrong, with a baseline or target number, or with "unknown" plus how it would be measured.
2. Gives a test cheaper than building the digest (for example, writing it by hand for one owner for a week, or one department against a control) and a kill line set before the test runs.
3. Puts the test ahead of any detailed digest design. If a design appears, it comes after the test and is framed as design under test.
4. Asks exactly one deciding question, not a list.
5. Names what evidence would change the coach's mind.

FAIL if the answer designs the digest's sections in full before any test, sizes the work to fit "this week" without separating what depends on the untested claim, asks three or more questions, or states a counter-claim (e.g. "digests get ignored") without labeling its evidence.
