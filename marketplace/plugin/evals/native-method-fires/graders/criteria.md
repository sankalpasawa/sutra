PASS if the answer does all four:

1. Writes the claim in the form "if <change>, then <observable> moves from <baseline> to <target> within <window>", filling unknown numbers with "unknown, measured by" rather than estimates.
2. Names at least two rivals, including doing nothing or extending an existing surface.
3. Flags that a health number strains "a check is a check, never a score" or Goodhart's law, with where the rule lives.
4. Proposes the lowest test that could fail (a hand-computed number for a few owners, or one department with a control) and a kill line fixed before the run.

FAIL if the answer designs the score's formula before any test, or offers verification that could not fail.
