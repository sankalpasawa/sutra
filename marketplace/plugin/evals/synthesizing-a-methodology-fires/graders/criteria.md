PASS if the answer does all four:

1. Scans at least three fields and names at least one formal result with its author or year (Kingman's formula, Little's law, the bottleneck law, requisite variety, or similar).
2. States whether the fields converge (science), one field applies (technique), or none does (guess), in those terms or a plain gloss.
3. Assembles the method as ordered steps, each tied to a named result and each ending on a check that could fail.
4. Says where the method gets placed, and that a placement needing a new altitude in Native waits for the founder's stamp.

FAIL if the answer derives a formula without naming the fields it came from, presents one field's result as settled science, or claims the method is canon without saying where it was placed.
