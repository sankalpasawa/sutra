# Q2: NG7 — non-technical users without terminal fluency

## Question

Verbatim per §14.10 row Q2 (post-2026-05-09 ratification): the row records the resolved answer in place of an open prompt. The original question shape was: should NG7 (non-technical users without terminal fluency) be a HARD non-goal for v1, or a soft non-goal revisitable mid-cycle?

## Why it matters

NG7 sets the surface ceiling for v1. HARD-ruling NG7 keeps v1 terminal-only (cheaper, faster, founder-aligned), but caps TAM. Soft-ruling NG7 opens pressure to author web/app surfaces before PMF, dilutes wedge focus, and risks pre-PMF surface sprawl.

## Default if unanswered

n/a — answer is recorded in canon row directly; no fallback default applies once ratified.

## Answer

NG7 is a **HARD RULE for v1**. Non-technical users without terminal fluency are explicitly out of scope. Revisit at v3 once terminal-CoS PMF is established and Q9 v2+ web/app surfaces come online. Holding NG7 hard preserves v1 wedge integrity and prevents premature surface investment.
