# Q1: Primary persona — founder-portfolio vs operator-class

## Question

Verbatim per §14.10 row Q1 (post-2026-05-09 ratification): the row records the resolved answer in place of an open prompt. The original question shape was: which persona is the v1 wedge — solo founder operating 1-N companies (terminal-fluent), or single-company operator-class (manager-IC)?

## Why it matters

The chosen persona sets every other product decision — surface (terminal vs web), tenancy model, approval shape, pricing unit, NG/anti-persona ruling, onboarding fidelity. Picking founder-portfolio first gives tighter feedback loops; picking operator-class first gives broader TAM but slower iteration cycles. Native cannot serve both as v1 — depth-of-judgment differs.

## Default if unanswered

n/a — answer is recorded in canon row directly; no fallback default applies once ratified.

## Answer

Primary persona = **founder-portfolio** (solo founder operating 1-N companies, terminal-fluent). Secondary persona = **operator-class** (single-company manager-IC). The v1 wedge validates the tighter-feedback primary; v2 expansion to secondary lands once PMF on the primary is established.
