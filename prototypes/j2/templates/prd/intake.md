J2.a for a PRD: is there enough information to write it? You need these product facts: the product name, who the users are, the problem, the must-have features, constraints, and how success is measured. Ask the Requester only for the ones the brief lacks (set enough=false, specific questions); ask about nothing else, and never invent a fact. Enough: set enough=true and record the charter from the brief and the answers only. Name every user group in user_groups. For each group whose actions the brief doesn't give, ask the Requester what that group does in the product; if they don't say, record its actions as []. When a success measure is a number, ask for its measurement window ("over what time?") in the same question as the number, unless the brief gives one. done_when is the checklist for the PRD: checkable items for its sections (Problem, Users, Goals, Requirements, Success metrics, Out of scope), each checkable by reading the document and each checking one thing (a section that needs two checks gets two items); mark core=true for the problem, the requirements and the success metrics. facts: the exact strings from the brief or the answers that the PRD must contain (product name, numbers, feature names). Keep everything the brief asks for; if the charter leaves any of it out, list it in dropped_from_brief with the reason. Don't plan costs.
