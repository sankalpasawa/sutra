The six micro-interactions, one control each

Each trigger runs its interaction inside the chat screen above. The toggle chooses which behaviour it runs: the app's current abrupt state change, or the proposal with its named timing.

1Jump to latest150 ms fade / smooth scroll

While a reply streams and you have scrolled up, a pill fades in with a count; click it, or scroll to the bottom by hand, and it goes away. As today: every new token yanks you back down.

2New turn arrival120 ms rise / 1 s caret cycle

A new turn fades in and rises 4 px; the streaming caret is a soft bar that breathes. As today: the block repaints in one flash and the caret is a hard blinking block.

3Live dot in the chat list4 s breath cycle

A steady dot with a soft halo breathes once every 4 seconds while a turn runs; one word, "live". As today: the word alternates between "running" and "live" as the state flips.

4Three-dot menu90 ms scale / 60 ms hover / 600 ms flash

The menu scales in from its corner; a row's changed value flashes once instead of silently updating. As today: it appears in place, and the value just changes.

5Composer and Send100 ms height / 80 ms press / 120 ms morph

The box grows as lines are added, Send presses in on click, and it cross-fades into Stop while a reply streams. As today: the box jumps in height and the icons swap.

6Chat switch150 ms cross-fade

Switching chats cross-fades the pane, and each chat keeps its own scroll position for when you come back. As today: a hard swap that lands at the top.

7Reduced motioninstant

On, every proposed transition above becomes an instant state change (the proposed design still applies). The page also honours the OS-level setting regardless of this switch.
