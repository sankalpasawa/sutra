Six chat micro-interactions, today beside proposed

Each row is one moment in the chat. The left panel shows what the app does today, the right panel the proposal. Both replay by themselves every 7 seconds; click a panel to replay it now. Nothing here is built yet.

How they would be applied: small additions to the chat screen's existing stylesheet and script, on the elements it already has. No new screens, no new colours; each one is a few lines.

1Jump to latest

When a reply is streaming and you scroll up to re-read something.

Today: the view stays where you put it, but nothing tells you the reply kept going below.

Proposed: a small pill appears at the bottom counting the new lines; click it and you are at the end.

2A new reply arrives

When the assistant starts answering.

Today: the caret at the end of the reply is a hard block that blinks on and off; the block pops in fully formed.

Proposed: the caret is a thin bar that softly breathes; the block eases in instead of popping.

3Live dot in the chat list

When a chat is working (a turn is running).

Today: a steady green dot with the word "live" (since this morning; it used to blink).

Proposed: the same dot; the ring around it swells and settles once every 4 seconds while the chat works, so "working" is visible without a blink. (Drawn larger here than in the app so you can see it.)
