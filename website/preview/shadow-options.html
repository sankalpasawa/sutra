Shadow — how it catches your eye

Four ways the overlay can ask for attention, each shown in three moments: needs you (a session dropped), low (a reply finished), and calm (nothing to say). Same app, same tokens. Rule for all four: one slow entrance, never a pulse.

A · Quiet corner the current design · MVP

A small dot lives above the composer. When it has something, one line appears beside it and leaves on its own.

D · Beside you needs Focus · M1

The dot stays in the corner, but the line appears next to where you are: under the composer while you type, beside the session card you’re looking at. Never over typed text, cursor, menus or a selection.

Recommendation: A when calm, B when it needs you, D once it knows where you are. A quiet dot is the anchor you can always find. The sunrise is how it catches your eye — light, not shape, one slow entrance, gone in 12 seconds, warmer when it matters more. When Focus ships, the same line moves beside your work instead of the corner. Peek stays as a setting for days you want nothing visible.
