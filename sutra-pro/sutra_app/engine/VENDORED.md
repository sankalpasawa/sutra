History, not a source: the earlier syncs took the J2 prototype at the commits `9200cf6` and `33aeeda` and `025e265c` (sync 4); the design documents cite the line numbers (`j2.py:n`) of `ce2631f`, and everything after `j2.py:8313` has moved. Patches are located by anchor text, never by line.

Copied into `sutra_app/engine/` with the same module names; every change to a J2 file is one of the numbered patches below and nothing else (decision D13). Prompts stay byte-identical.
